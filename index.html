Welcome to Fantasy College Football!

  1. Pick 5 Teams

    Each week, choose 5 college football teams to add to your lineup.

  2. Teams Score Fantasy Points

    Your teams earn points based on how they perform in their games.

  3. Matchups Matter

    Each team's score is adjusted based on the strength of its opponent. Beating a strong opponent can be more valuable than beating a weak one.

  4. Manage Your Picks

    You can add or remove teams until that team's game starts. Once their game begins, that team is locked in for the week.

  5. Climb the Leaderboard

    Your weekly points are added to your season total. See how you stack up against everyone else!

That's it. Pick smart, watch the games, and climb the leaderboard!
